Dawnhub Advertiser Tools

Dawnhub Advertiser Tools is a Chrome extension for Dawnhub advertisers. It adds a few tools directly to the Dawnhub raids and bookings pages.

What it does

Fill booking from a DM

Paste a message from a buyer, as a screenshot or text, to fill in the raid, Name-Realm and price of a booking. More

Find a buyer

Search the raids in the dates you're viewing for a character's bookings. More

Raids with slots available

Hide raids that are already full from the raids list. More

Raid time links

Open a raid in a new tab by middle-clicking its time. More

Data access

The extension only runs on Dawnhub and only talks to Dawnhub, using the access your account already has. It never submits anything on your behalf. Screenshots and messages you paste into it are processed on your computer and are not uploaded. See the privacy policy for details.
