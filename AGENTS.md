## Privacy is part of the contract

This extension is published publicly with a privacy policy (`hosted/privacy.html`) and Chrome Web Store data
disclosures that promise the extension talks only to Dawnhub, that anything pasted into it never leaves the device,
and that list exactly what is stored and where. Any change to what the extension reads, stores, or sends - including
a new feature - must update the privacy policy in the same change, and must be called out so the store listing's disclosures can be updated before release.
